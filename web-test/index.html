  1. Open in Safari over https, then Share → Add to Home Screen.
  2. Launch from the Home Screen icon, not Safari. Check "Display mode" says standalone.
  3. Tap the button. Allow the mic. Note whether a dialog appeared.
  4. Close it fully (swipe up from app switcher). Reopen from the icon. Tap again.
  5. Repeat 3–4 times, then reboot the phone and do it once more.

"Times it asked" counts launches where granting took over 400 ms — roughly, a dialog you had to tap. Instant grants mean iOS remembered. Trust what you actually saw over the number if they disagree.
